About

A small team with a long memory for systems.

We started Jettx because too much software is delivered and then abandoned. We wanted to build the other kind.

What we believe

Good engineering is mostly about reducing the number of surprises. That shows up in boring places: readable code, reproducible environments, honest estimates, and a support arrangement that exists before the first incident rather than after it.

How we operate

  • One accountable lead per engagement. You always know who to call.
  • Written status, every cycle. Progress, risks and decisions in one place.
  • No lock-in by obscurity. Documentation and access are yours from day one.
  • We say no. If we are not the right team for the work, we will tell you early.
